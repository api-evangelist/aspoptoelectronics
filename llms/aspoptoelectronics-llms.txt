# Acquirezy

> Acquirezy is a company, deal, and contact intelligence platform for investors, dealmakers, and researchers. It covers 5.4M companies, 200K+ M&A acquisitions, 700K+ funding rounds, 327K investors, 2M+ people, and 61M professional contacts — all searchable, filterable, and free to browse on the web.

Data is free to explore. Paid plans (Pro $9/mo, Intelligence $29/mo) unblur funding/investor/web-traffic/contact data, add CSV export, contact unlocks, acquisition email alerts, and Chase AI.

## Datasets
- [Companies](https://acquirezy.com/datasets/companies): 5.4M companies — industries, HQ, employees, founding year, funding, IPO/stock, web traffic, technology, patents, and signal scores.
- [Acquisitions](https://acquirezy.com/datasets/acquisitions): 200K+ M&A deals with acquirer, target, price (disclosed for ~15% of deals), type, and date.
- [Funding Rounds](https://acquirezy.com/datasets/funding_rounds): 700K+ rounds with amounts, valuations, stages, and investors.
- [Investors](https://acquirezy.com/datasets/investors): 327K VCs, PE firms, accelerators, and angels — portfolio, exits, and stages.
- [People](https://acquirezy.com/datasets/people): 2M+ founders and executives with roles, companies, and investment activity.

## Browse by entity
- [Company profiles](https://acquirezy.com/organization/google-1177816): per-company M&A insights (totals, trends, top target sectors, biggest & latest deals), funding rounds, and full company details.
- [Sectors](https://acquirezy.com/sector/software): acquisitions, funding, and active investors within an industry.
- [Years](https://acquirezy.com/year/2024): the biggest M&A deals, largest funding rounds, and most active acquirers of a year.
- [Directory](https://acquirezy.com/directory): index of all companies, sectors, and years.

## About
- [Pricing](https://acquirezy.com/#pricing): Free browsing of all data; Pro ($9/mo) and Intelligence ($29/mo) for export, unlocks, alerts, and Private Equity M&A Intelligence.
- [Chase AI](https://acquirezy.com/chat): ask natural-language questions about M&A patterns, companies, funding, and investors (paid plans).
